Disc

National Spinal Decompression Certification

  • Since2012
  • Edition14th, October 2026
  • CE12 hours
  • Held atParker University, then National University, now Life University
  • Exam80% to pass

The certification started at Parker University in 2012, with Parker as its first sponsor and CE provider. It has since been held at National University and now at Life University, where the October 2026 edition sends its proceeds back to the university.

How it works

It's equipment-neutral: doctors learn the protocol, not a brand of table. Certification takes a written exam with an 80% pass mark, and the program is approved by the International Medical Advisory Board on Spinal Decompression, ten medical doctors and ten chiropractors.

The textbook

The Ultimate Spinal Decompression Book, more than 840 pages of interviews and protocols, reviewed by Chiropractic Economics and The American Chiropractor in 2023.

Nerve

Peripheral Neuropathy Certification

  • 2019Chiropractic Peripheral Neuropathy Certificate, Parker University
  • 2026Peripheral Neuropathy-Spinal Decompression Certificate, Life University
  • CE8 hours (2026)

In May 2019, Dr. Kaplan and Dr. Bard taught the Chiropractic Peripheral Neuropathy Certificate at Parker University in Dallas, described at the time as the first university-based neuropathy CE program, with Dr. Michael Putman.

In February 2026 it grew into the first-ever Peripheral Neuropathy-Spinal Decompression Certificate, taught live at Life University. Eight hours, co-taught with neurologist Dr. Jay Lombard and Dr. Salvatore LaRusso, Chairman Emeritus of the NBCE.

The textbook

The Ultimate Peripheral Neuropathy Book, with interviews and protocols from Dr. Lombard.

A full day at Life University's continuing education center, taught by Dr. Kaplan and Dr. Bard with Dr. Salvatore LaRusso, Dr. Marty Kotlar and Dr. Jason Kaplan.
