Dr. Kaplan earned his Doctor of Chiropractic in 1978 and built his practice to six clinics seeing more than 200 patients a day.

Then he took that experience into business.

"I became president and chief operating officer of a public company that owned and operated Nutrisystem."Dr. Eric Kaplan, The American Chiropractor, June 2024

He brought that business experience back to the profession. Today he co-chairs the International Medical Advisory Board on Spinal Decompression and leads the International Disc Educational Association, and he teaches the certifications he and Dr. Bard run at Life University.

Alongside the clinical textbooks he wrote with Dr. Bard, he has written books on health and performance for general readers, and a column for the basketball site Daily Knicks.

Dr. Bard is a Life University graduate who built his practice to more than 20 new patients a day and kept it there four years running.

He went on to build five multi-million-dollar clinics, and retired from practice at 40.

What he's known for now is the communication: how a doctor explains a condition so the patient understands it, chooses care and stays with it.

"We don't cure things. What do we do? We control and we contain. It is so much in the language. It is so much in the communication."Dr. Perry Bard

In 2019 he co-created the Chiropractic Peripheral Neuropathy Certificate at Parker University, described at the time as the first university-based neuropathy CE program. With Dr. Kaplan he co-hosted The Physician Spotlight Radio Show, interviewing doctors from the network, and he writes for The American Chiropractor, including his column "All Roads Lead to Disc."

Life University · 27 February 2026

A DISC Centers of America clinic inside a chiropractic university.

In February 2026, Life University opened the first-ever Life University DISC Centers of America Outpatient Center. The protocol Dr. Kaplan and Dr. Bard built is now part of how Life trains chiropractors.

  1. In the curriculum. DISC Centers of America protocols are built into Life's chiropractic program, and students are certified before they graduate.
  2. A working clinic on campus. The outpatient center opened with three spinal decompression tables, with a fourth planned, plus decompression and non-surgical knee systems.
  3. Certifications held at Life. The Neuropathy-Decompression Certificate (February 2026), the Non-Surgical Knee Care Certificate (October 2026) and the National Spinal Decompression Certification are all taught on campus.
  4. Giving back. Proceeds from the October 2026 certification go back to Life University.
