What doctors learn

The protocol itself, independent of any brand of table. How to recognize the disc patient, how to stage a condition from phase one to phase four, and how to explain it so the patient understands the plan of care.

Who reviews it

The program is approved by the International Medical Advisory Board on Spinal Decompression, ten medical doctors and ten chiropractors, including orthopedic surgeons, neurosurgeons and neuroradiologists.
